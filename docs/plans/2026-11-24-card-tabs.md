# Card tabs, keep warm in the chip, pictures for every figure

Status: plan, agreed in conversation on 2026-11-24 from mockups. Not started.

## Why

- The open card shows too much at once. The user reads PACE VS RESET, DELEGATION and the spend; the
  rest (cache, keep warm, the metric row) is not used or not understood.
- On a wide band (4 card columns) the metric row never fits under the 12-row cap, so CACHE HIT,
  PER TURN, BY MODEL and the new TOOL OUTPUT cell are never seen. Seen in the app: `open card 1+0+9 of
  12 rows, card 4 cols`.
- Numbers alone are hard to read: every figure should have a picture next to it.

## What it becomes

**Top row.** As today: the usage pies, the cost, Robo and the WARM/COLD chip with minutes left. Keep warm
becomes a flame-only button right after the chip (🔥 on, 🧊 off) at every width; the word "Keep warm"
goes. Its figures (cost per hour, what going cold costs, what it saved) move into the chip's hover
(`<title>` in the chip's SVG, which is drawn `isInteractive`) and the card's alt text.

Checked in the host types: a `Svg` cannot take a press (presses go on a `Button`, whose children are text
only), so the chip itself cannot be the switch. The button next to it is the switch.

**Tabs.** While the card is open, tab buttons sit in the top row before `−`: names on a wide band,
one-glyph buttons on medium and narrow bands. The chosen tab is kept in the store (as the progress
style is) and also set with `/spendometer tab overview|context|cost`. Default: Overview.

| Tab | Shows | Pictures |
|---|---|---|
| Overview | Pace vs reset, delegation, this thread's spend with commits and PRs, today and the week | Pace bars with the time tick (today), delegation's cost and time bars (today), 7 day bars (today) |
| Context | How full the conversation is and what filled it | One stacked bar: setup, chat, tool output by tool; a gauge of turns left; file chips for re-reads |
| Cost | Where the money goes | A bar per message for the last 10 (an outlier in red, the last in orange); a split bar for the cache discount; warm against cold as two bars; cost by model as today's split bar |

Names say what a figure means: "Cache hit" becomes "Cache discount", "In cache" becomes "Conversation
size", "Per turn" becomes "Per message".

**Height.** No tab is taller than the card is today. Pace stays on Overview only. The metric row goes;
its cells move into Context and Cost. The narrow band's tab row is the one extra row, and `fitOpen` must
count it.

**Widths.** `cardColsOf` gives 1 to 4 columns. Each tab defines its 3, 2 and 1 column forms (4 draws as 3
wider ones). At 1 column a tab draws its main picture and puts the rest in status lines, as the card does
today.

## New data

All on the session record (`Spent`), carried by `readSpend` and `recordOf`, kept small:

- `recent: number[]`: the last 10 main-turn costs, for the per-message bars.
- `ctxFirst: number`: the context size after the first priced main turn. It estimates "setup" (system
  prompt, tools, CLAUDE.md) in the stacked bar. An estimate: say so in the alt text.
- `rereadFiles: Record<string, number>`: re-reads per file name (basename), capped at the 5 most recent
  files.

## Ship order (one visible change at a time, each seen in the app before the next)

1. **Keep warm in the chip.** Flame-only button after the chip, the hover text in the chip, the KEEP WARM
   column and its status line removed. The cold state keeps saying what a re-cache costs.
2. **Tabs, with today's pieces.** Tab buttons, the stored choice, the command. Overview as described;
   Context and Cost first reuse today's IN CACHE zone and the metric cells, so the TOOL OUTPUT cell becomes
   visible at once. The metric row goes.
3. **Context pictures.** The stacked bar (needs `ctxFirst`), the gauge, the file chips (needs
   `rereadFiles`).
4. **Cost pictures.** The per-message bars (needs `recent`), the discount bar, warm against cold.
5. **README and mockups.** `assets/mockups.sh` draws each tab at wide and narrow, light and dark; the
   README's card section describes the tabs. Hold the mockups commit from 2026-11-24 (four-cell metric row)
   until then: it shows a row that goes away.

Each step: tests first, `BUN_INSPECT= claude plugin test .` and `claude plugin validate .`, the user
checks it in the app with `/reload-plugins --force`, then commit to `dev`.

## Open questions

- **The terminal.** It has no SVG: it draws the card's text form. Tabs there: the same buttons, the
  pictures as the text fallback the card already has. Check what that form draws before step 2.
- **Hotkeys for tabs.** Buttons take one digit or letter. Digits answer a survey in an empty composer,
  so letters (`o`, `c`, `$` is not allowed: maybe `m` for money). Decide in step 2.
- **Narrow tab row.** One extra row, or one button that steps through the tabs. The mockup uses the row;
  revisit if the cap drops the progress row too often.
